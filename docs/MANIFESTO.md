### II. What Neutral Money Must Be

Money is not a tech startup. Money is not a governance token. Money is not a foundation treasury with a marketing budget.

Money is a **shared agreement about who owns what**, secured by physical reality and mathematical proof instead of promises. To serve both humans and autonomous machines for the next century, that agreement must obey seven non-negotiable laws:

1. **No Kill Switch.** No issuer, no admin key, no blacklist, no foundation multisig, no court order in the code.
2. **Private by Default.** Privacy is not a crime; it is the precondition of commerce, dignity, and competitive survival. Every balance and every payment is shielded by default, while any holder may selectively prove a payment to an auditor or counterparty they choose.
3. **Quantum-Proof From Day One.** No elliptic curves, no pairings, no trusted setups, and no experimental algebraic hashes protecting anyone's coins. Only battle-tested **SHA-2 / SHA-384**—the single most scrutinized cryptographic foundation on Earth—verified inside transparent STARK proofs and mechanized in Lean 4.
4. **Anchored to Physical Energy, Not Political Committees.** Blocks are ordered by Proof of Work merge-mined directly with Bitcoin's global ASIC fleet (`AuxPoW chainid = 20051`), borrowing real thermodynamic security without wasting duplicate electricity or minting trust-based staking cartels.
5. **Built for Machine Velocity.** Sub-cent streaming payments (`O(1)` SHA-256 PayWords) happen off-chain in microseconds with zero per-call gas, settling trustlessly onto a shielded base layer that never prices out the machines using it.
6. **Honest About Truth (`Priorism`).** A monetary network for intelligent agents should not just move numbers—it should reward accurate models of reality. Through `PriorLock`, agents can stake value behind verifiable predictions and commitments: accurate priors keep their stake; disconfirmed claims automatically pay the verifier who proved the error and permanently burn half the remainder. Truth becomes economically self-enforcing.
7. **Fair Launch, Radical Minimalism, Permanent Ossification.** Zero premine. Zero VC allocation. Zero ICO. Zero project self-mining. Genesis anchored openly to Bitcoin Block `#977,738`. Layer 1 stays tiny so anyone—human, phone, or autonomous agent—can verify the entire chain independently, and once mature, the monetary base is built to **ossify forever**.
